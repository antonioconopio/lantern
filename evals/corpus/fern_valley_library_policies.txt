FERN VALLEY PUBLIC LIBRARY: CIRCULATION AND FACILITY POLICIES

HOURS
Monday to Thursday: 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
The library is closed on Sundays and statutory holidays.

LIBRARY CARDS
Library cards are free for residents of Fern Valley. Non-residents may purchase a card for $60 per year.

BORROWING LIMITS
An adult card may have up to 25 items checked out at a time. A child card may have up to 10 items checked out at a time.

LOAN PERIODS
Books: 21 days.
DVDs: 7 days.
Laptops: 3 hours, for use inside the library building only.

RENEWALS
Items may be renewed up to 2 times, unless another patron has placed a hold on the item.

OVERDUE FINES AND LOST ITEMS
There are no overdue fines on children's materials.
Overdue adult materials are charged $0.25 per day, up to a maximum of $10 per item.
A lost item is charged at its replacement cost plus a $5 processing fee.

MEETING ROOMS
Meeting rooms can be booked up to 30 days in advance. Each booking may last up to 3 hours.
