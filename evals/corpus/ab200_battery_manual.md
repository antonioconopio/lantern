# Aurora AB-200 Home Battery: Owner's Manual

## Specifications

- Usable capacity: 11.2 kWh
- Continuous power output: 6 kW
- Peak power output: 9 kW for up to 10 seconds
- Weight: 112 kg
- Operating temperature: -10°C to 45°C
- Charging is disabled below 0°C. The battery can still discharge between -10°C and 0°C.

## Installation

The AB-200 must be installed by a certified Aurora installer. Leave at least 30 cm of clearance on each side of the unit for airflow, and do not install it in direct sunlight.

## Operating modes

- **Backup mode** reserves 100% of capacity for power outages.
- **Self-Powered mode** is the default. It powers your home from stored solar energy and keeps a backup reserve of 20%, which you can change in the app.
- **Time-Based mode** charges from the grid during off-peak hours and discharges during peak hours to reduce your bill.

## Error codes

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E01 | Grid disconnected | No action needed. The battery switches to backup power automatically. |
| E04 | Over-temperature | Output is reduced automatically until the battery cools below 40°C. |
| E07 | Communication fault | Restart the gateway by holding its button for 10 seconds. |
| E12 | Cell imbalance | Contact Aurora support. Do not restart the battery more than twice. |

## Updates

Firmware updates install automatically at 2:00 a.m. local time. The battery keeps powering your home during an update.

## Warranty

The AB-200 is covered for 10 years or 6,000 full charge cycles, whichever comes first. Aurora guarantees the battery will retain at least 70% of its original capacity during the warranty period.
